# Mindset Academics 🎓
 
> An online tutoring platform built to help students unlock their potential through personalised tutoring sessions.
 
---
 
## 🚧 Status
 
This project is in the **early idea/planning phase**. We're currently scoping the platform and deciding on a tech stack.
 
---
 
## 💡 About
 
Mindset Academics is an online tutoring platform connecting students with tutors who emphasise not just academic knowledge, but the growth mindset skills needed to thrive — confidence, resilience, and independent thinking.
